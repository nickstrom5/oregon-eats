Terms of use

The plain-language terms for the Oregon Eats app and this website. Last updated 2026-10-24.

What the app is

Oregon Eats is a free guide to restaurants in Oregon. It is provided as is, for personal use, without charge and without warranties of any kind.

Check before you go

Restaurants open, close, change their hours and change their menus, food carts move, and many coast places keep seasonal hours. Dishes and founding years are what each place or a named source said when we checked in fall 2026. We work to keep the lists right, but we can't promise that any listing is current or complete. Call the restaurant before you make the trip.

Public records

Liquor licenses come from the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission's list on the state's open data portal, shown with the license type, number and expiration date OLCC publishes; licensee names are left out. Oregon publishes no restaurant inspection results in bulk, so the app and this site show none and compute no grade. A missing license means unknown, not unlicensed. For official records, ask the agency itself.
