Oregon brewpubs

266 places in Oregon with an active Brewery Public House license from the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission: breweries that pour their own beer and serve food on site.

Straight from the license

OLCC's Brewery Public House license lets a brewery pour its own beer at a pub that serves food. This list comes from that license, not from a review or a reader poll: each place here holds one, matched to it by the license's trade name.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Portland (73), Bend (26), Eugene (18), Hood River (9), Salem (7), Hillsboro (5), Roseburg (5), Cannon Beach (5).

Our method: every place in our data matched to an active OLCC Brewery Public House license (read October 24, 2026), one place per license, chosen by the license's trade name. Nothing here is hand-checked or ranked by a score, and a brewery that pours under another license type isn't on this list. Hours change, so check before you go.

On your phone, Oregon Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
