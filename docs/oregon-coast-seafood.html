Oregon coast seafood

15 places on the Oregon coast for clam chowder, Dungeness crab and oysters from Netarts, Tillamook and Coos bays, each checked in fall 2026.

Chowder weather

Dungeness crab, clam chowder and bay oysters are the coast's everyday seafood: crab boats work out of Newport and Astoria, and oysters grow in Netarts, Tillamook, Yaquina and Coos bays. Many coast places keep shorter hours in winter.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Newport (5), Astoria (2), Coos Bay (1), Seaside (1), Depoe Bay (1), Tillamook (1), Yachats (1), Manzanita (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a source named it and its street address matched our records. A dated 2025 or 2026 page (local news, an official tourism listing or the place's own site) was enough; an older or undated page counted only when the place also held an active liquor license on the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission's list in 2026. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.
